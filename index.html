About Lakshmi Flour Mill

A local mill built on trust.

Lakshmi Flour Mill has been serving customers since 1997. Our aim is simple: provide dependable grinding services for everyday household and food-preparation needs, while respecting the traditional care that makes a local mill special.

From grains and flour to chillies and kolamavu, we welcome customers looking for friendly service and careful handling.

  • Established in 1997
  • Flour milling and grain grinding
  • Chilli grinding and kolamavu
  • Convenient local service in Chinnakavanam
